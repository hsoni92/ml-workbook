# Principles of Marketing — Revision Mind Map

> Everything important, in acronyms and one-liners. Pair with `sample-paper-01.md` and the speculative papers for exam patterns.

## Marketing Foundations

```
MARKETING = Acquire + Engage + Build Relationships + Create Value
ONE-LINER = "Providing value to final consumers"
```

- **Value Equation** — $\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Benefits}}{\text{Costs}}$ (perceived, subjective)
  - $>1$ high value → loyalty · $=1$ fair value → neutral · $<1$ overpaid → churn
  - Numerator lever: add features/quality/experience/warranty · Denominator lever: lower price or **non-monetary cost** (time, effort, friction — Amazon one-click)
- **Tangible value** = Economic (price/efficiency) + Functional (utility/performance)
- **Intangible value** = Social (status/belonging) + Experiential (emotion/ambience) → **more inelastic demand** = pricing power
- **Need / Want / Demand** — $\text{Demand} = \text{Want} + \text{Ability} + \text{Willingness to pay}$
  - Marketers **cater to needs**, **shape wants** (never "create needs"), **convert** wants into demand via price & perceived value
- **Demand function** — $D_x = f(P_x, i, P_y, T, E)$; law of demand: price ↑ → quantity ↓ (ceteris paribus)
- **Elasticity** — $E_d = \frac{\%\Delta Q}{\%\Delta P}$
  - $|E_d|>1$ elastic (wants/luxuries): price ↑ → **revenue ↓** · $|E_d|<1$ inelastic (needs): price ↑ → **revenue ↑** · $=1$ unitary: revenue flat
  - Strategy: shift elastic → inelastic via intangible/experiential value (generic coffee → Starbucks)
- **Digital Mix = EOP** — **E**arned (reviews/shares/press; highest credibility) · **O**wned (website/blog/email/app; you control) · **P**aid (PPC/display/ads; fastest). Loop: Paid → Owned → Earned
- **Relationship Groups** (2×2: loyalty × profitability) — **Strangers** (L/L: minimal investment) · **Butterflies** (H/L: short-term promotions) · **True Friends** (H/H: invest heavily, loyalty) · **Barnacles** (L/H: upsell to improve margin)

## Strategy Frameworks

- **AIDA** — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action; flywheel extends with **Confidence → Satisfaction** (loops back)
- **PESTEL** — Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental (external macro scan)
- **Porter's Five Forces** — Rivalry, Supplier power, Buyer power, Substitutes, New entry (industry competitiveness)
- **SWOT** — Strengths (I/Helpful) · Weaknesses (I/Harmful) · Opportunities (E/Helpful) · Threats (E/Harmful)
- **STP** — $Segmentation + Targeting = Positioning$
  - Segmentation divides (bases: Geographic, Demographic, Behavioural, **Psychographic**) → Targeting selects (undifferentiated/differentiated/concentrated; evaluate on size, profitability, reachability) → Positioning defines mental image
- **Positioning** — Product-oriented ("we sell coffee") vs Market-oriented ("third place") → market-oriented wins (emotion, less commoditisable, premium)
  - Statement = **Target + Frame of reference + Point of difference (POD) + Reason to believe**
- **BCG Matrix** (relative share × market growth) — **Stars** (H/H: invest) · **Cash Cows** (H/L: milk/harvest) · **Question Marks** (L/H: invest or exit) · **Dogs** (L/L: divest)
- **Blue Ocean vs Red Ocean** — Red: fight for existing demand, choose differentiation **OR** low cost. Blue: create new demand via **Value Innovation** (differentiation **AND** low cost; e.g., Cirque du Soleil). Blue oceans turn red as imitators arrive
- **Perceived Risk** — 6 types: **Financial, Psychological, Performance, Time, Social, Physical** → reduce with refunds, trials, testimonials, certs, convenience, identity/CSR
- **Involvement** — Low → Routine/habitual (Diet Coke); High → Extended problem-solving (car/house). Modes: **Routine / Limited (impulse) / Extended**

## Research, Analytics & Behaviour

- **Primary vs Secondary research** — Primary: firsthand, owned, tailored, costly, slow. Secondary: existing, shared, cheap, fast. Best practice: **both** (secondary = landscape, primary = depth)
- **Analytics Maturity** — **Descriptive → Diagnostic → Predictive → Prescriptive** (what → why → will → how)
- **Statistics** — Descriptive (mean/median/mode, range, variance, SD, IQR) vs Inferential (A/B test, p-values, confidence intervals). Sampling: **Stratified** (accurate, reliable, inefficient) vs **Cluster** (efficient, less accurate)
- **7Ps** — Product, Price, Place, Promotion + **People, Process, Physical Evidence** (extended Ps critical for services)
- **ABC Behaviour Model** — **A**ntecedents (triggers) → **B**ehaviour (action) → **C**onsequences (reward)
  - Don't know you exist = weak A · arrive but don't buy = hard B (friction) · buy once never again = weak C
- **Copywriting vs Content** — Copy: immediate action, short, persuasive, conversion metric. Content: educate/trust, long, narrative

## Branding

- **Brand** = name/sign/symbol + promise + perception built from experiences (NOT just logo/colour — those are assets)
- **Six levels of meaning** — Attributes → Benefits → Values → Culture → Personality → **User**
- **Consumer roles** — identify source, learn from experience, simplify decisions, reduce risk
- **Firm roles** — tracing, legal protection (trademark/patent), quality signal, demand security, entry barrier, **~20–25% price premium**
- **Assets vs Vehicles** — Asset = identity element (Coke bottle, brown colour, slogan) · Vehicle = market expression (trucks, billboards, sponsorships)
- **Brand Architecture Pyramid** (base→peak) — Features → Functional benefits → Emotional benefits → Personality → **Core/Essence** (IndiGo: on-time/low-cost → "dream of every Indian flying")
- **Brand Development Grid** (brand × category) — **Line extension** (same/same: Diet Coke; risk cannibalization) · **Brand extension** (same/new: Dove Men+Care; risk dilution) · **Multibrand** (new/same: Unilever shampoos; risk internal rivalry) · **New brand** (new/new: clean slate; risk high build cost)
- **Maslow** — physiological → safety → belonging → esteem → self-actualization (a lens, not a rigid ladder)

## Pricing

- **Pricing zone** — floor = **costs**, ceiling = **perceived value**, strategy in between
- **Cost-based** (product → cost → price → convince) vs **Value-based** (perceived value → target price → allowable cost → design) vs **Competition-based** (above/below/parity; price-war risk)
- **Skimming** (high→low over time; needs quality image + early adopters) vs **Penetration** (low start for share/scale; often raises later)
- **Price Discrimination** — 1st degree = individual WTP (surplus → 0; haggling/dynamic pricing) · 2nd degree = self-select by quantity/version · 3rd degree = observable segments by elasticity (student/senior discounts)
- **Two-part tariff** = fixed access fee + variable usage fee (gyms) · **Bundling** = package value (McDonald's meal)
- **CLV/LTV** — $\text{CLV}_{net} = (\text{avg revenue} \times \text{lifespan}) - \text{cost to serve}$; forward-looking; same price ≠ same CLV (retention decides)

## Metrics & Money

- **CAC** — $\frac{\text{Sales and marketing spend}}{\text{New customers}}$ (same-period rule)
- **LTV:CAC** — ≤1 danger · ~**3:1 healthy** · ≥5 may mean underinvesting
- **Payback** — $\frac{CAC}{AMR \times GM}$ (don't forget gross margin!)
- **Churn** — $\frac{\text{customers lost}}{\text{customers at start}} \times 100$ · **ARPU** — $\frac{\text{revenue}}{\text{users}}$
- **NMC** — Net sales − COGS − marketing expenses
- **Marketing ROS** — $\frac{NMC}{\text{Net sales}}$ (profit share; distinguishes equal-revenue campaigns)
- **MER** — $\frac{\text{Total revenue}}{\text{Total ad spend}}$ (≈3–4 healthy; system-wide view when attribution hard)
- **Market share** — $\frac{\text{company revenue}}{\text{industry revenue}} \times 100$ (revenue) · $\frac{\text{company units}}{\text{industry units}} \times 100$ (volume) · **Relative share** = $\frac{\text{company sales}}{\text{leader sales}}$ ($>1$ = ahead of leader)

## Digital Advertising

- **Meta/Google Ads structure** — Campaign → Ad group → Ads (goals → type → targeting → budget)
- **Key metrics** — **CPC** (cost/click) · **CPM** (cost/1,000 impressions) · **CTR** (clicks/impressions) · **CPA** (cost/action/conversion) · **ROAS** (revenue/ad spend)
- **Quality Score** ≈ landing page ~39% + expected CTR ~39% + ad relevance ~22%
- **Bidding** — maximise conversions / target ROAS / manual CPC (AI bids preferred; use Keyword Planner range)
- **Performance marketing = investment banking** — allocate capital by risk/return; review 2–3 weeks of data; watch CPC, CTR, CPA together
- **GA metrics** — users, sessions, medium (organic/paid/social/email/referral), key events (conversions)
- **Attribution models** — First click (awareness) · Last click (closer) · Linear (equal) · Time decay (recent-heavy) · **Position-based U-shape (~40/20/40)** · Data-driven (ML). Changing the model changes credited conversions

## Execution & Thinking Models

- **Waterfall vs Sprinkler** — Waterfall: sequential, high-value first, low risk, needs time (enterprise SaaS). Sprinkler: all segments at once, fast, high cost, needs capital (fashion/games)
- **Sales vs Marketing** — Marketing creates & nurtures demand (MQLs, long horizon); Sales converts to purchase (SQLs, closed revenue, short cycle). Sales is *part of* marketing
- **Intro pricing grid** — Rapid/Slow Skimming (high price) × Rapid/Slow Penetration (low price) combined with high/low promotion
- **Product Lifecycle** — Introduction (low sales, negative profit) → Growth → Maturity (peak) → Decline (harvest/exit)
- **Thinking models** — **Map ≠ Territory** (models are maps, not reality) · **5 Whys** (root cause) · **IUR** (Ignorance → Uncertainty → Risk; move unknowns to measurable risk)
- **Storytelling** — **PPT** = Place, Protagonist, Time + conflict + resolution; inverted pyramid = most important first
- **Ethics** — false wants & excessive materialism debate; responsible/sustainable marketing; tap Maslow's *stable needs* ethically
