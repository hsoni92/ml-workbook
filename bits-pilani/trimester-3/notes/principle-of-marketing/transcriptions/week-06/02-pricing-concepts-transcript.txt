Welcome back. In this video we are
going to take a deep dive into one of
the most essential concepts in pricing
what's price? Simply put, price is the
amount of money charged for a product
or a service, or, more importantly, the
total value that consumers are willing
to exchange for the benefits they
receive from using it. It's important
to remember that costing and pricing
are distinct concepts. Costing is a
reality. It tells you what you must
cover to produce or deliver your
product. On the other hand, pricing is
a strategy. It's how you position your
offering in the market to meet your
business goals and customer
expectations.
