Hi again. In this video we're going to
compare price and promotion across 3
key dimensions. First, when we look at
the primary role, price determines the
monetary value of the exchange, while
promotion communicates and persuades
consumers. Next, in terms of impact on
the bottom line, price directly
influences revenue and profit margins.
Promotion, however, is usually an
expense focused on long term growth
rather than immediate profit. Finally,
under consumer perception, Price shifts
how consumers view the worth or status
of the product, whereas promotion
influences the awareness and
desirability of the product. So in
summary, price captures value, while
promotion creates communication and
demand.
