Hi everyone. Welcome to the final
module of this marketing course. I hope
you have been enjoying this journey so
far. Over the past 12 weeks and modules
we have covered many core ideas in
marketing. We begin with understanding
what marketing is, then explore demand,
consumer behaviour, psychology,
segmentation, branding, pricing, and
even distribution. Now in this module
we bring several of those ideas
together and then apply them to real
marketing decisions. Let me briefly
walk you through the three areas we
will focus on this module. The first
area is advertising budgets. Every
marketing team must decide how much
money to invest in acquiring or
retaining or engaging with customers.
To make that decision responsibly,
marketeers rely on various metrics such
as customer acquisition cost, lifetime
value, payback period, and even market
share models. The second area focuses
on ethical marketing. Marketing
influences behaviour and consumption,
so it's important to examine debates
around materialism, responsible
communication, and sustainable
practises. The third area is career
readiness. We'll focus on practical
skills such as resume building,
marketing interview preparation, and
professional positioning as we move
deeper into the module. We will also
pay special attention to how these
concepts integrate in real world
decision making. Modern marketing is
increasingly data-driven, and budget
decisions today rely not only on
intuition but on measurable performance
indicators. Companies that can
correctly interpret their acquisition.
Data churn patterns and long term value
projections are able to scale faster
and make more confident and strategic
choices. Throughout this module, we'll
break down simple examples from real
business scenarios so you can exactly
see how these tools are applied. Beyond
those, we will also highlight the
importance of marketing in a social
environment. Brands today operate in an
environment where customers expect
transparency, fairness, and even
responsibility. Ethical concerns such
as privacy, inclusiveness, and
environmental impact are becoming
central to how companies build trust.
Understanding these dynamics will help
you become a great marketeer who not
only drives growth but also contributes
positively to the society. Lastly, we
will also guide you through developing
a strong Professional marketing
identity, good marketing careers are
built on communication skills,
strategic thinking, and the ability to
adapt. By mastering both technical and
professional skills, you will be much
better equipped to thrive in an ever
competitive marketing world. Now let us
look at the learning objectives for
this module. By the end of the module,
you will be able to define and apply
key marketing metrics such as CAC, LTV,
and marketing return on sales, which
help on advertising decisions. You
should also be able to evaluate
marketing strategies from an ethical
and sustainability perspective. Finally,
you should also understand how to
prepare for marketing careers by
developing the skills and professional
awareness required in the workplace.
