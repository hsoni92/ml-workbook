Now let's look at another important concept in branding which is called as brand personality. What is brand personality? Brand personality is the point where a brand becomes humanized. It refers to a set of human traits and characteristics that's defined how a brand behaves and communicates. When a brand feels human, people are able to connect more and more naturally and emotionally with that brand. Now that you have understood what brand personality means, let's explore how it can be measured.
Social psychologist Jennifer Aker identified five key dimensions used to measure brand personality. First is sincerity. Then the second parameter is excitement. The third is competence. The fourth is sophistication. And finally the parameter is ruggedness.
Now let's understand the first dimension which is sincerity. A sincere brand feels genuine and pure heart. at it. It communicates honestly with warmth and joyfulness. Examples include Disney, Amazon or Cadbury.
The next trait of brand personality is excitement. An exciting brand feels adventurous, energetic, vibrant. It often appears creative, bold and modern. Examples Coca-Cola, Nike, Red Bull, Tesla.
The third dimension is competency. A competent brand is seen as trustworthy, intelligent and reliable. It reflects expertise, accomplishment, and efficiency. Examples include Microsoft, Google.
Next, we have sophistication. A sophisticated brand feels elegant, luxurious, and highass. It often represents prestige and refined taste. Examples could include like Gucci, Apple, Tiffany, and Rolex.
Finally, we have ruggedness. A rugged brand feels outdoorsy, strong and resilient. It projects toughness and durability in its personality. Examples could include Timberland, Jape, Malbero or Harley Davidson.
