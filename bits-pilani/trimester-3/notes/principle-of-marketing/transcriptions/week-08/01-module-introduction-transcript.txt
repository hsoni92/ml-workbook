Hi everyone and welcome to week 8. In
this session, we'll continue building
on the foundations of meta ads as we
explore the core concepts that make up
the meta ads ecosystem.
This module is designed to help you
gain a deeper understanding of meta ads,
which is an essential tool for digital
marketing across platforms like
Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. By
the end of this module, you will be
able to describe the foundational
basics of me ads, including the
structure and the role of ads within
the broader marketing landscape. You
will also learn to explain the
different ad formats such as image ads,
video ads, carousal ads, and lead
formats, and also understand their
specific purposes. Each of these
formats serves a unique role in helping
you achieve various marketing
objectives, from raising brand
awareness to driving conversion. In
addition to exploring these ad types,
we will dive into how you can choose
the most effective ad format based on
your goals and target audience. You
will learn how to create those ads that
resonate with the users, whether you're
aiming for a high engagement, lead
generation, or just simply product
promotions.
