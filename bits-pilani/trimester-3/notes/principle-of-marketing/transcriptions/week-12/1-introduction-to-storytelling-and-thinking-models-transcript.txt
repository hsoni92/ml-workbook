Hello and welcome to week 12 of a
learning journey. I'm really glad
you're joining me for this session on
exploring storytelling and thinking
models. This week we will explore two
skills that sit at the heart of
powerful communication and strong
decision making. No matter your role or
experience, these skills have the
potential to transform the way you
share your ideas, influence people, and
solve problems. Let's begin with the
big picture. Every day in the workplace
we are surrounded by information, data,
reports, presentations, metrics, and
decisions, but information alone isn't
memorable. It doesn't naturally create
connection or clarity. This is where
storytelling comes in. Stories get
structured to scatter thoughts and
transform complex ideas into messages
people can understand, relate to, and
recall. They help you make your point
in a way that feels human. At the same
time, even the most compelling story
needs to be built on strong thinking.
That's where thinking models support us.
Thinking models are not just
complicated theories, they're simple,
proven ways to analyse situations,
break down problems, and help you make
better decisions. When used together
with storytelling, they give you both
clarity in understanding an issue,
clarity in communicating the solution.
This dual model brings the best of the
two worlds together in a practical,
approachable way. You'll get the tools
you can immediately apply, not just in
presentations, but in meetings,
strategy discussions, and day to day
workplace conversations. So what's the
learning objective? By the end of this
video, you will achieve two key
learning objectives. We will unpack
what makes our story great in the
business environment. This includes
understanding how to identify your
audience, craft your emotional hooks,
structure a message that flows
naturally, and present ideas that
resonate. You will learn simple
storytelling techniques that transform
a plain update to a persuasive
narrative. You will also see examples,
some from companies, some from real
world storytellers, to help you
understand how these elements work in
practise. You will learn how to look at
the problems through a structured lens
that make complex topics easier to
understand. These models, such as first
principles thinking, second principles
thinking, inversion thinking, and more,
allow you to move beyond surface level
observations and think more deeply,
more logically and even more creatively.
We'll walk through each model in simple
language and then explore how to apply
them in your work as they become part

confident using them. So let's get
started. Let's explore how the right
story is built on the right thinking
that can change the way you communicate
