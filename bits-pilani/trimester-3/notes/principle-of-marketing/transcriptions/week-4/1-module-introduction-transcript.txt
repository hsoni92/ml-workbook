Hi everyone. Welcome to the module on
marketing fundamentals and creative
execution. In this session we will
explore the 4 and 7 pieces of marketing,
examine effective copywriting styles,
tactics, and practical hacks, and then
dive into design basics and visual cues.
We will wrap up by understanding user
behaviours and how they influence
marketing decisions. Before we get
started, let's take a moment to
understand the learning objectives from
this module. By the end of this session,
you will be able to understand the 4
P's and 7 Ps of marketing. Explain high
level ideas behind commonly used
copywriting styles, designs, and visual
cues. Also analyse and compare how user
behaviour changes over the time.
