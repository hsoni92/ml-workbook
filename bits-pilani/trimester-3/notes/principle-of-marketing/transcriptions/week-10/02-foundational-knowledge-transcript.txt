Understanding the different types of
app campaigns and targeting options
available will allow you to tailor your
approach and achieve your app's
marketing goals. So let's get started.
When it comes to app campaigns, the
strategy you choose will depend on
specific goals you have for your app.
Google Ads provides the three main
types of app campaigns, each with its
unit's objectives app campaigns for
instals, app campaigns for engagement,
and app campaigns for pre-registration.
Let's break each one down. First up, we
have A campaign for instals. These
campaigns are also known as ACI. They
are designed to encourage people to
install your app. Whether you have just
launched your app or you're looking to
boost its presence in the marketplace.
ACI campaigns are your go to option.
The main goal here is to reach users
who are likely to download and install
your app. Google Ads uses machine
learning to automatically optimise your
ads and show them to people who are
most likely to take action of
installing your app. ACI campaigns are
great for targeting a broad audience,
but you will also want to think about
narrowing your targeting to specific
groups that are more likely to find
value in your app, such as people who
have similar app usage behaviours or
interests. Next, we have app campaigns
for engagements, or ACE. Once your app
has gained a little bit of traction and
you have users who already have
installed your app, the goal often
shifts from accusation to engagement.
ACE campaigns help you re-engage who
have already installed your app but may
not be using it as actively as you'd
like them to do. For example, if your
app has users who haven't purchased
anything in a while, or users who have
abandoned their shopping carts, AC
Campaigns allows you to target those
users specifically. You can also
encourage in-app purchases for your
products or services, or even send them
reminders about a live event or sale
happening in the app.
To create an AC campaign, however, your
app needs to have at least 50,000
instals. This is because AC campaigns
rely on having a substantial user base
to effectively target and re-engage the
right people. With ACE, Google Ads
utilises machine learning and AI to
optimise your campaigns, ensuring your
ads are shown to the right users at the
right time, leading to higher
engagement and retention rates. Now
let's talk about app campaigns for
pre-registration, which are specific to
Android apps only. This type of
campaign is designed to help you build
excitement and anticipation for your
app before it's officially released on
Google Play Store. If you're planning
to launch a new app or game, a
pre-registration campaign can help you
generate interest and sign ups in
advance. These campaigns work by
promoting your app or game to users who
are likely to pre-register for it. By
targeting users who have shown interest
in similar apps or games, or who have a
history of pre-registering new releases,
app campaigns for pre-registration
ensure your app has an eager audience,
ready and waiting when you finally
launch. Pre-registration campaigns can
also help you gather valuable insights
on how your audience is responding to
your app or game before it's released.
Giving you the opportunity to fine tune
your marketing strategy ahead of time.
However, keep in mind these campaigns
are only available for iOS apps. One of
the most powerful features of app
campaigns is the ability to target
specific audience segments based on
their behaviour and interest. Google
Ads offers a variety of audience
targeting options that can help you
reach the right people at the right
time. Let's take a look at some of the
targeting options that are available
for your app campaigns. Here are some
of the audience targeting options that
are available. Firstly, detailed
demographics. With this, you can target
user groups based on long term life
facts. These demographics can include
age, gender, household income, parental
status, and more. This is a great way
to narrow down your audience, down to
the people most likely to use your app
based on personal characteristics and
life stages. For example, if your app
is related to parenting or child
education, targeting parents in
specific age ranges might be especially
effective.
Then we have life events. Life
event-based targeting allows you to
reach users who are going through
important milestones or life changes.
This could include getting married,
buying a home, having a baby, or even
graduating. These significant life
moments often influence people's
behaviours and needs. So if your app
offers services or products related to
these events, this is a powerful way to
target your audience at the right time.
With affinity targeting, you can reach
users based on their passions, habits,
and interests. Google Ads uses a vast
amount of data to group users to
affinity categories such as sports
enthusiasts, travellers, foodies, and
more. If your app aligns with any of
these interests, this is a great way to
reach a highly engaged audience that is
likely to download and use your app.
Then we have in-market targeting
focuses on reaching users who have
shown. Recent intent to make a purchase
or take specific action. For example,
if your app is an online shopping
platform, you can target users who are
actively looking for products in your
category. In-market targeting helps you
focus on users who are more likely to
take the next step. Whether it's
downloading your app, making a purchase,
or engaging with your content. In
addition to these targeting options,
you can also create audience segments
based on specific actions users have
taken within your app. For example, you
can create segments of users who have
completed a purchase, started a signup
process but didn't finish, added items
to their cart but didn't check out. By
creating these segments, you can target
users more effectively, sending
tailored messages that encourage them
to take the next step. Whether that's
