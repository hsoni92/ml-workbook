## Case Study Assignment

### Decoding the Quick Commerce Growth for [X]

Students can choose a quick commerce company of their choice (e.g., Blinkit, Zepto).

**Topic:** Hyperlocal Dominance, Dark Store Economics, and Brand Defensibility in India's Quick Commerce Sector.

---

## 01: Executive Summary & Category Definition

Students must first define the **Points of Parity (POP)** and **Points of Difference (POD)** between Quick Commerce, Traditional E-commerce, and Modern Trade.

- **The "Why Now":** Analyse the CAGR and market growth rate in India.
- **The 2026 Landscape:** Acknowledge the current market positions (e.g., Blinkit, Zepto, etc.).

---

## 02: Choose [Company X] and Run Market Analysis for It

Students can choose a quick commerce company of their choice (e.g., Blinkit, Zepto).

**Market Analysis (PESTEL & Porter's):** Perform a brief scan using PESTEL and Porter frameworks (e.g., gig worker regulations, fuel price volatility).

---

## 03: Run Situational Analysis — SWOT Matrix

Identify:

- One **internal Strength** (e.g., Zepto's 10-minute surgical efficiency)
- One **external Threat** (e.g., tough competition or shrinking margins)

---

## 04: Strategic Frameworks (STP & The 4Ps)

### Segmentation & Targeting

Define the **"Priya" persona** (24-year-old urban professional).

- Is the brand using **differentiated** or **concentrated** targeting?

### Positioning

Contrast the product-oriented definition ("We deliver groceries") with a market-oriented one ("We offer instant gratification / time freedom").

Explain the **4Ps** and **7Ps** in this context.

---

## 05: Infer Brand Strategy & Consumer Behaviour

### Infer Brand Personality

Using **Aaker's dimensions**, does your brand denote **excitement** (vibrant/modern) or **competence** (trustworthy/smart)?

Define the **brand architecture** for your company of choice.

### The ABC Model

Diagnose a friction point. If "people visit the app but don't check out," is the behaviour too hard (high friction/UX) or is the antecedent (the push notification) not urgent enough?

### Perceived Risk Modeling

How does your brand "de-risk" a high-value purchase (e.g., a smartphone on Blinkit)?

- Identify the **financial risk**
- Identify the corresponding **de-risker** (e.g., a robust refund policy)

---

## 06: The Growth & Profitability Challenge

**The task:** Propose a strategy to increase **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** without relying on deep discounts.

**Pricing strategy:** Propose a move in the current pricing strategy. Example: from penetration pricing to value-added pricing or third-degree price discrimination (e.g., loyalty-based "Zepto Pass" vs guest users).

---
