## Retail fundamentals (Week 1)

```
RETAILING = selling goods/services to FINAL consumers for personal/household use
NOT retail = manufacturer → wholesaler (B2B bulk)
```

| Format | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Store-based | Supermarket, department, specialty |
| Non-store | E-commerce, mail, vending, direct selling |
| Non-traditional | Pop-up, subscription boxes |

**Ownership** — corporate chain, franchise, cooperative, independent

**Value chain** — retailer links producer and consumer; creates **assortment, convenience, service**

**Consumer behaviour** — needs → wants → **demand** (want + ability + willingness); motivation theories (Maslow, etc.)

**India context** — unorganized → organized retail; digital + quick commerce; **omnichannel**

## Retail strategy (Weeks 2, 7)

- **Strategic analysis** — market, competition, positioning, capabilities
- **Financial lens** — margins, inventory turns, working capital, P&L drivers
- **Location** — trade area, footfall, catchment, cannibalization
- **Technology** — POS, CRM, loyalty, supply chain visibility
- **McKinsey 7S** — strategy, structure, systems, shared values, style, staff, skills — alignment audit
- **KPIs** — sales/sq.ft, same-store growth, shrinkage, conversion (where measurable)
- **Retail audit** — systematic review of strategy execution vs plan

---

## Merchandise and inventory (Week 3)

- **Assortment planning** — width (categories) vs depth (SKUs); seasonal lifecycle
- **Inventory objectives** — right product, place, time, quantity; avoid stock-out and overstock
- **Buying** — forecasts, lead time, MOQ, vendor terms
- **Markdowns and clearance** — lifecycle end; margin vs cash recovery
- **GMROI** mindset — gross margin return on inventory investment (analytics weeks)

---

## People and operations (Weeks 4–5)

- **Store organization** — roles, spans of control, accountability
- **HR strategy** — hire, train, schedule, incentives linked to store KPIs
- **Operations** — receiving, stocking, planograms, checkout, returns
- **Management decisions** — staffing vs traffic, security, shrink, compliance, local vs central policies

---

## Communication and promotion (Week 6)

- **Retail image** — positioning, ambience, consistency across touchpoints
- **Promotion mix** — advertising, in-store, personal selling, loyalty, digital
- **Customer relationship** — acquisition expensive; **retention** lowers average acquisition cost

---

## Retail analytics with Excel (Week 8)

- **Descriptive** — sales by store/category/time; pivots, trends
- **Diagnostic** — why did KPI move? decompose drivers
- **Forecasting** — moving averages, seasonality (course extends in later weeks)
- **Pricing / margin** — unit economics, promo lift (where covered in notes)
- **Data hygiene** — clean keys, dates, handle returns before aggregating

## Exam traps

- **Wholesaling ≠ retailing** — final consumer test
- Strategy without **inventory cash** angle — overstock locks working capital
- **Same-store sales** vs new stores — growth mix matters
- Promotion lift without **baseline** — same mistake as CRO without control group
- 7S — all seven must **align**; fixing one S while others conflict fails
